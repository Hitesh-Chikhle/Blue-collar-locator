Design Thinking Project Concept

Blue Collar is an applied academic inquiry tackling urban fragmentation in unorganized local service markets.

1. Empathize & Define

Target personas: Students in PGs with urgent leaking taps, corporate workers returning late facing tripped circuit breakers, and senior residents seeking trusted mechanics without predatory charges.

2. Ideate & Prototype

A responsive zero-overhead web platform using native web standards, direct geocoding, live availability toggles, and dual-mode data synchronization.

3. Ethical Architecture

No dark patterns, no inflated fake reviews, strict demonstration labels, and privacy preservation via client-side storage fallbacks.

Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Are the listed technicians real?

No. As this is an academic prototype for college evaluation, all service technicians are fictional demo profiles to test booking flows safely.

How does real-time synchronization work without a backend?

We employ a dual-adapter pattern: when Firebase credentials are configured in js/config.js, Firestore onSnapshot is utilized; otherwise, the app seamlessly falls back to BroadcastChannel + localStorage for multi-tab live sync.

How do I test both Customer and Worker roles simultaneously?

Open two browser windows or tabs. Set one to Customer and the other to Professional. Any presence updates or simulated bookings will sync instantly across both!

Privacy Policy (Academic Prototype)

This website runs entirely on client-side vanilla JavaScript. Location coordinates obtained through browser Geolocation are retained strictly in your browser session for distance calculations via OpenStreetMap and are never transmitted to third-party ad networks or sold.

You may clear your browser's LocalStorage at any time to purge all local mock bookings and preferences.

Terms of Academic Use

Blue Collar is constructed strictly for academic, design thinking, and educational demonstration. No actual monetary payments are processed, no emergency services are dispatched to physical homes, and all credentials are demo representations.

Emergency notice: For real-world life-threatening emergencies, dial 112 directly.
